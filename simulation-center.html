Purposeful Learning Environments

Realistic spaces for meaningful healthcare learning

The Simulation Learning Center gives students opportunities to practice clinical decision-making, patient communication, teamwork, and technical skills before entering real healthcare environments.

01

Emergency Care Bay

Students respond to urgent patient scenarios while practicing assessment, communication, safety, and coordinated team response.

02

Hospital and Patient-Care Bay

A realistic patient-care setting supports instruction in vital signs, positioning, mobility, hygiene, documentation, and bedside communication.

03

Skilled Nursing and Examination Bay

Students develop foundational healthcare skills while learning professionalism, dignity, infection control, and patient-centered care.

Student Learning

From classroom knowledge to clinical application

A

C

Communicate

Practice respectful patient interaction, teamwork, handoffs, documentation, and professional communication.

R

Apply classroom learning to realistic scenarios and make thoughtful decisions in a structured, supportive environment.

R

Reflect

Review performance, discuss decisions, learn from mistakes, and identify ways to improve future patient care.

The Simulation Process

Practice, make decisions, reflect, and improve

Simulation creates a safe place for students to apply their knowledge. Scenarios can be paused, discussed, repeated, and adapted as students develop new levels of competence and responsibility.

Preparation

Students review the patient situation, learning objectives, equipment, safety expectations, and assigned team roles.

Simulation

Students participate in realistic scenarios requiring clinical skills, communication, decision-making, and teamwork.

Debriefing

Students reflect on what happened, explain their decisions, receive feedback, and plan how to improve.

Preparing students for healthcare education and careers

The Simulation Learning Center connects academic content, technical skill development, career exploration, and professional expectations in one active learning environment.
