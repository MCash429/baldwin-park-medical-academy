Hi Braves — it's Ms. Cash! Welocome to the new Alumni Network! I have given my heart to BPHS, and as a teacher graduate I am so proud to work alongside you as an Honorary Alumni. 💙

First things first: there is no pressure here. Joining this network does not mean you have to speak to a class or mentor anyone — unless you want to, and that would be amazing! Mostly, I just want us all to stay connected. We will have special events both on and off campus where you can meet up, network with other Braves, and honestly just have a great time together. As an hoalum, I will be right there with you.

And if somewhere down the road you do want to help a current student — a conversation, a job shadow, a word of encouragement — that door is always open. Our students need to see people like you and know what is possible. But showing up for each other as alumni is just as valuable.

I will personally be reaching out to everyone who joins. Please sign up — it takes two minutes, and it means the world to me. I cannot wait to reconnect with you. 💙

Ways to give back

  • 🗣️
    Share your story An honest account of your path — college, training, challenges, turning points — helps students see what's possible.
  • 🤝
    Mentor one-on-one Answer questions, review college applications, or talk through a career pathway with a student who needs guidance.
  • 🎤
    Speak or demonstrate Visit a class, join a career panel, or demonstrate a clinical skill that students won't see anywhere else.
  • 🏥
    Host a job shadow Give students a real look at your workplace — nothing inspires a career choice like seeing the work firsthand.
  • 💙
    Support the academy Help with equipment, scholarships, certifications, internships, or connections to healthcare partners.

🎉 Alumni Events
