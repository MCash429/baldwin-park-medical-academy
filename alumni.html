A Living Legacy

Every graduate has a story that can change a student’s future.

Some alumni knew their direction early. Others changed majors, found a new specialty, balanced work and college, served their communities, or took an unexpected route into healthcare. Those honest stories help today’s students see that there is more than one path to a meaningful career.

Where Braves Serve

A network across the full healthcare community

The academy will highlight graduates at every level of education and career development—from students beginning college to experienced professionals, educators, researchers, and community leaders.

🩺

Medicine & Nursing

Physicians, nurses, nurse practitioners, physician associates, medical assistants, and patient-care professionals.

🏃

Rehabilitation & Sports Medicine

Athletic trainers, physical therapists, occupational therapists, chiropractors, and rehabilitation specialists.

🚑

Emergency & Public Service

EMTs, paramedics, firefighters, emergency department staff, military medical personnel, and public-health professionals.

🔬

Science & Allied Health

Researchers, laboratory professionals, imaging specialists, pharmacists, dentists, therapists, and healthcare educators.

Build the Network

There are many ways to come back and make a difference

Alumni involvement can be as simple as answering a few student questions or as substantial as mentoring, hosting a workplace visit, speaking to a class, or helping strengthen the academy.

01

Share Your Story

Tell students about your education, career, challenges, turning points, and the advice you wish you had received.

02

Mentor a Student

Offer encouragement, answer questions, review goals, or help a student understand a college or career pathway.

03

Speak or Host

Visit a class, join a panel, demonstrate a skill, host a workplace tour, or participate in an academy event.

04

Support the Academy

Help with equipment, scholarships, certifications, internships, networking, or connections to healthcare partners.

A Network With Purpose

Connect the academy’s history to its future

Over decades, students have moved from Baldwin Park classrooms into colleges, hospitals, clinics, athletic programs, emergency services, laboratories, schools, and community organizations. This page creates a home for those relationships instead of allowing them to disappear after graduation.

  • Show students people and pathways they can identify with.
  • Create meaningful career and college conversations.
  • Reconnect graduates with teachers, classmates, and the academy.
  • Develop mentors, speakers, workplace partners, and supporters.
  • Celebrate the long-term impact of Baldwin Park education.
