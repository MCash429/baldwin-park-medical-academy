Explore Our Programs

Four connected learning tracks

Students move from scientific foundations to real-world healthcare skills, simulations, certifications, and career preparation.

🧠

Anatomy & Physiology

Study body systems, medical terminology, injury, disease, and the scientific foundations of patient care.

🏃

Sports Medicine

Learn injury prevention, evaluation, emergency care, rehabilitation, prehabilitation, and athletic training skills.

❤️

Patient Care

Practice vital signs, infection control, communication, patient safety, professionalism, and essential care skills.

🚑

Emergency Response

Prepare for CPR, AED, Stop the Bleed, first aid, splinting, wound care, and team-based emergency response.

The Simulation Learning Center

Practice in realistic healthcare environments

Students develop clinical judgment, teamwork, communication, and confidence through structured scenarios in emergency, hospital, skilled-nursing, sports medicine, and rehabilitation settings.

  • Emergency and trauma scenarios
  • Hospital and ICU patient care
  • Skilled nursing and vital signs
  • Sports medicine and rehabilitation
