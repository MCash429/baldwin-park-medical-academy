# Baldwin Park Medical Academy Online

## Preparing Tomorrow's Healthcare Professionals

Welcome to the Baldwin Park Medical Academy Online project.

This repository contains the development of an interactive learning platform designed to prepare high school students for careers in healthcare through engaging lessons, simulation-based learning, and real-world experiences.

---

## Vision

To create one of the nation's premier digital healthcare academies for secondary education.

---

## Mission

Empower students with the knowledge, skills, confidence, and compassion needed to become tomorrow's healthcare professionals.

- Knowledge
- Skills

- Leadership
