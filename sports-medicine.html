Career Technical Education

Sports Medicine Pathway

Students develop healthcare knowledge, practical clinical skills, professional habits, and a stronger understanding of careers in sports medicine and related health professions.

Explore the Pathway View College Credit

Pathway Highlights

  • Hands-on learning in a dedicated Sports Medicine Learning Center
  • Clinical skill development using industry-standard equipment
  • Authentic work-based learning experiences
  • Professional certifications in BLS and Stop the Bleed
  • Up to six units of articulated college credit
  • Leadership and competition through SkillsUSA

Why Choose Sports Medicine?

Build knowledge and skills that connect the classroom to healthcare careers.

Students examine the structure and function of the human body, learn how injuries occur, and study the principles used to prevent, evaluate, and care for common injuries.

The pathway also places strong emphasis on communication, teamwork, safety, professionalism, and responsible decision-making. These skills prepare students for further education and a wide range of healthcare careers.

  • Apply anatomy and medical terminology in practical situations.
  • Develop injury-prevention and patient-care skills.
  • Practice professional communication and teamwork.
  • Learn through demonstrations, simulations, and hands-on activities.
  • Explore college programs and careers in healthcare.

About the Pathway

A connected sequence of learning experiences.

Students move from foundational knowledge to more advanced applications while developing confidence, technical skills, and professional responsibility.

🫀

Anatomy and Physiology

Students study body systems, medical terminology, movement, health, injury, and the relationships between structure and function.

🩺

Sports Medicine

Students apply foundational science to injury prevention, evaluation, immediate care, rehabilitation principles, and healthcare practice.

🤝

Career Preparation

Students strengthen communication, leadership, collaboration, reliability, and other habits expected in college and the workplace.

Sports Medicine Learning Center

A dedicated environment where students can practice skills, apply course content, and learn to use equipment commonly found in sports medicine and rehabilitation settings.

Hands-On Learning

A dedicated space for practice, application, and skill development.

The Sports Medicine Learning Center supports active learning and gives students opportunities to move from explanation and demonstration to guided practice.

  • Injury evaluation and observation activities
  • Protective taping and wrapping techniques
  • Rehabilitation and therapeutic exercise principles
  • Emergency response practice
  • Patient positioning and mobility activities
  • Documentation and professional communication

Clinical Skills

Students learn through purposeful practice.

Instruction emphasizes safety, accuracy, communication, and understanding—not simply completing a procedure.

1

Injury Prevention

Students examine risk factors, conditioning, protective equipment, body mechanics, and strategies that may reduce injury.

2

Injury Evaluation

Students practice observation, history-taking, basic assessment, and communication within the appropriate scope of a high school learning environment.

3

Taping and Wrapping

Students learn the purposes, limitations, safety considerations, and techniques used in common supportive applications.

4

Emergency Response

Students practice recognizing emergencies, activating the response system, providing immediate care, and working as part of a team.

5

Rehabilitation Principles

Students study range of motion, strength, balance, flexibility, progression, and safe return-to-activity concepts.

6

Professional Communication

Students practice listening, asking appropriate questions, documenting information, protecting privacy, and communicating respectfully.

Work-Based Learning

Authentic experiences with healthcare professionals.

Work-based learning helps students understand how classroom knowledge, technical skills, communication, and professional behavior are used in real settings.

Certified Athletic Trainers

Students learn from Certified Athletic Trainers and observe how professionals make decisions, communicate, organize care, and respond to injuries.

Authentic Experiences

Students participate in structured activities that connect classroom learning to the expectations and responsibilities of healthcare workplaces.

Professional Habits

Students develop reliability, teamwork, confidentiality, respectful communication, initiative, and attention to safety.

College Credit Opportunities

Earn articulated college credit through Mt. San Antonio College.

Students who meet course requirements and pass the appropriate articulation examination earn college credit.

MEDI 90

Medical Terminology

The Anatomy and Physiology course is articulated with Medical Terminology at Mt. San Antonio College.

3 college units

KIN 19

Care and Prevention of Athletic Injuries

The Sports Medicine course is articulated with Care and Prevention of Athletic Injuries at Mt. San Antonio College.

3 college units

Students who pass both articulation examinations earn a total of 6 college units.

Professional Certifications

Students can earn recognized emergency-care credentials.

Certification instruction strengthens readiness, confidence, teamwork, and the ability to respond appropriately during an emergency.

♥

American Heart Association Basic Life Support for Healthcare Providers

Students learn high-quality CPR, use of an automated external defibrillator, team-based response, and care for choking emergencies.

✚

Stop the Bleed®

Students learn how to recognize life-threatening bleeding and provide immediate care using direct pressure, wound packing, and tourniquet application.

Student Leadership

Develop leadership, teamwork, and career-readiness skills through SkillsUSA.

SkillsUSA gives students opportunities to strengthen communication, leadership, technical knowledge, and professional confidence.

Through chapter activities, service, leadership development, and competitive events, students can demonstrate what they have learned while building connections with classmates, educators, and industry professionals.

Career Exploration

Sports medicine can lead to many different healthcare pathways.

Students explore the education, responsibilities, working conditions, and skills associated with careers across healthcare, fitness, rehabilitation, and human performance.

Athletic Trainer Physical Therapist Physical Therapist Assistant Occupational Therapist Physician Physician Assistant Registered Nurse Emergency Medical Technician Exercise Physiologist Strength and Conditioning Specialist Rehabilitation Specialist Other Allied Health Careers

Career exploration helps students identify personal strengths, understand educational requirements, and make informed decisions about college, certification programs, and future employment.

From Baldwin Park to Healthcare Careers.

The Sports Medicine Pathway gives students a place to discover their interests, develop practical skills, earn college credit, and begin preparing for the next step.
